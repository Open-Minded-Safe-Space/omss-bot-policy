Privacy Policy

OMSS Redemption Bot

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the OMSS Redemption Bot may process information when used in the Open Minded Safe Space (OMSS) Discord server.

1. Information We Process

The bot may process the following information as needed for its functions:

  • Discord user IDs to identify members making redemption requests.
  • Server roles to verify eligibility and manage redemption passes.
  • Redemption status to prevent duplicate or unauthorized claims.
  • Messages containing supported commands, such as !Redeem1000.

The bot is not designed to request Discord passwords, payment card details, or authentication tokens from members.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Check whether a member has a valid redemption pass.
  • Process and track redemption requests.
  • Prevent duplicate or unauthorized redemptions.
  • Manage relevant server roles and rewards where supported.
  • Diagnose errors and maintain the bot.

3. Storage and Retention

Depending on the bot's implementation, redemption records or related information may be stored to prevent repeated claims and maintain reliable operation. Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes.

Members may contact an OMSS administrator to ask what information is stored and whether an eligible deletion request can be fulfilled.

4. Third-Party Services

The bot operates through Discord and may interact with other services, including Noctaly, if those integrations are configured. Discord and any connected services process information under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Sharing of Information

OMSS does not intend to sell members' personal information. Information may be processed by Discord or configured third-party services as necessary to operate the bot. Access to any stored records should be limited to authorized administrators.

6. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect bot credentials and any stored redemption records. Members should never share their passwords or authentication tokens with the bot.

7. Your Questions and Requests

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or information handled by the bot, contact an OMSS administrator through the server's designated support channel.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the bot's functionality or information-handling practices change. The latest version will be published at this webpage.
